# Lifecycle taxonomy

Use this configurable macOS-first default under the user's chosen local root:

```text
00 - Entrada
10 - Projetos ativos
20 - Areas
30 - Referencias
40 - Registros
90 - Concluidos para arquivar
99 - Controle
```

- **Entrada:** temporary triage only; leave unclear items at the source when moving them adds no value.
- **Projetos ativos:** finite work with a desired outcome.
- **Areas:** ongoing responsibilities such as clinical work, teaching, research, technology, administration, finance, personal life, family, and media.
- **Referencias:** reusable source material that does not belong to one active project.
- **Registros:** finalized records that must remain retrievable.
- **Concluidos para arquivar:** projects the user explicitly marked complete and that await external copy and verification.
- **Controle:** catalogs and human-facing indexes, not sensitive run manifests.

Do not create every possible subject folder up front. Add a subject only after real reviewed items justify it.
